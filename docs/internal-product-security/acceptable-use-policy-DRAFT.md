# LIBRIS Acceptable Use Policy — INTERNAL DRAFT

This policy should be adapted into user-facing rules before public uploads, reviews, comments, or sharing are enabled.

Users must not use LIBRIS to:
- access or disclose another person's private account, notes, reading history, or uploaded files;
- upload malware or content they do not have the right to use;
- distribute unauthorised copies of copyrighted books;
- harass, threaten, impersonate, or target other users;
- post spam, scams, unlawful material, or content prohibited by the final community rules;
- evade quotas, abuse automated endpoints, or disrupt the service;
- attempt to bypass security controls or obtain privileged access.

## Enforcement design
- Provide a clear reporting route.
- Restrict staff access to reports and private data.
- Define proportionate actions and a review/appeal process where appropriate.
- Record moderation actions without exposing reporters unnecessarily.
- Do not build community reporting/moderation screens until backend permissions and privacy behaviour are implemented.
- Before public sharing, publish community guidelines, reporting rules, and a copyright complaint process.
